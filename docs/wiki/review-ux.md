# Review UX

How the app leads a reviewer through a tour, beyond its look (the look is "Ink & Trail", built on the `impeccable-ui` branch).

On 2026-11-10 the maintainer asked for UX, not only style, and approved bolder changes of behaviour and layout. What changed and why:

- **One table of shortcuts.** `viewer::Command` holds every key the app answers, with its keys, its scope and the line the shortcuts sheet shows. `app::bind_keys` binds from it with one exhaustive match, and the sheet (`?`) reads the same table, so a key cannot be bound without being listed. Main buttons show their key caps from it. The three hint lines at the foot of the sidebar went; the sheet replaces them. New keys: `n` (next unread step), `m` (mark the step read or unread), `?` (the sheet). The palettes' arrows and return stay their own `on_key_down`, named in their footers. Commit 8d3baef.
- **Actions in the palette.** cmd+k lists places under Steps, Files and Symbols, the group with the best match first, then Actions that match by name; a query that starts with `>` lists only actions. An action does what its button or key does; its key cap shows when it has one. Why: reviewers reach for one box for both going and doing (Raycast, Linear). Commit 8d3baef.
- **One gap row.** The two stacked "Show more lines" rows between changes became one row that says how many lines are hidden, with up, down and show-all buttons (`viewer::HiddenLines`). Show-all adds the hidden count to one side's context. The end of a file keeps one direction and no count: the shown lines do not tell how long the file runs on. Commit 8d3baef.
- **The summary is a start page.** Counts the review truly has (steps, files changed, added and removed lines, open threads; no time estimate), the summary, the route of top-level steps with their why, read mark and check flag, and one lead: Start the tour, Continue at the first unread step, or, once every step is read, finish. Commit 283aa9f.
- **A finish moment.** Once every step is read, the last screen's footer and the summary's lead are a success card with Finish review, which opens the sidebar's form with the keys in its body. Where the PR takes no review (the viewer's own PR with nothing to send) the card says the tour is read and offers no button. Commit 283aa9f.
- **Toasts** (`viewer::Toasts`, drawn by `toast_stack`) for outcomes that were silent or easy to miss: a comment or reply added or posted, a thread resolved or unresolved, a review sent, Settings saved, lines copied, and a problem that was not there before. They leave after four seconds unless the pointer rests on the stack, three at most; failures stay until dismissed. A queued comment, an edit and a reaction show in place and get no toast. No failure toast has a try-again: the existing try-again controls are all in place where the failure shows. The shelf lives in a global so the toast of a Save survives the reopen that follows. Commit 283aa9f.
- **Settings is a sheet over the whole window** when a PR is open: the tour sidebar is under the scrim, not live beside it, and the palette, the switcher and the shortcuts sheet wait while it is open. Esc, the close button and a scrim click go through `request_close`, which asks before discarding. This removed the 2026-11-10 rule from da86e6a that a sidebar click while Settings is open closes it and goes there, asking first when unsaved: it had no trigger left, so `go_from_sidebar`, `ask_before_leaving` and the panel's after-discard callback went. The Settings model and its tests did not change. The Settings-only window still shows Settings as its whole page, with a pinned header. Commit 283aa9f.

Known open: hover and live motion were checked only in still pictures (`scripts/snapshot` runs with reduce motion); the sticky step bar's slide and the toasts' rise were not seen moving.
